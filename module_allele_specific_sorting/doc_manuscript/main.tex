\title{Locus-specific ancestry sorting across hybrid populations}
\author{}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

We tested whether strongly differentiated genomic regions show coordinated ancestry sorting across 20 hybrid populations and whether sorted loci are embedded in unusually long ancestry blocks. These empirical analyses describe the observed pattern but do not establish its cause; comparison with neutral simulations remains pending.
